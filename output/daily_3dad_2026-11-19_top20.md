## 1. Fuse Before Reconstruct: A Novel Reconstruction Paradigm for Multimodal Industrial Anomaly Detection（32 分）

- **命中**：摘要命中「3d-ad」+4；摘要命中「point cloud」+4；标题命中「industrial anomaly」+10；摘要命中「mvtec」+4；摘要命中「eyecandies」+4；有开源代码 +6
- **arXiv**：[2611.24541v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24541v1)
- **提交日期**：2026-11-18
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **Comment**：Preprint before peer review. Includes supplementary material. Code: https://github.com/LiZhi-CASIA/FBR
- **摘要**：Industrial anomaly detection plays a pivotal role in precision manufacturing, where joint analysis of multimodal data emerges as a critical approach to enhance detection accuracy. Feature reconstruction, a dominant paradigm for such joint analysis, is primarily implemented through intra-modal and cross-modal methods, both of which are plagued by significant limitations. Intra-modal reconstruction neglects complementary cross-modal information, while its cross-modal counterpart is prone to projection errors and ambiguous one-to-many mappings (e.g., same textures mapping to multiple geometries), leading to detection failures. To address these limitations, we propose a novel feature reconstruction paradigm, Fuse Before Reconstruct (FBR), which first fuses multimodal features and then performs reconstruction for anomaly detection. Firstly, we propose a bidirectional cross-modal attention mechanism to achieve deep feature complementarity between image and point cloud features, mitigating projection bias through channel alignment and dynamic spatial prior integration. Besides, a learnable pixel-wise gated fusion strategy is adopted to preserve multi-modal structural consistency while optimizing local feature combination. Finally, dual-path collaborative reconstruction is implemented to recover original modal features from the fused representations, where cross-modal consistency constraints effectively eliminate mapping ambiguities. Extensive experiments show that FBR achieves 98.3% I-AUROC on MVTec 3D-AD and 92.6% I-AUROC on Eyecandies, surpassing previous state-of-the-art methods by +2.9% and +4.1%, respectively.

## 2. AggCLIP: Aggregating CLIP for Zero-Shot Multimodal Anomaly Detection（26 分）

- **命中**：摘要命中「3d-ad」+4；摘要命中「point cloud」+4；标题命中「multimodal anomaly」+10；摘要命中「mvtec」+4；摘要命中「eyecandies」+4
- **arXiv**：[2611.24643v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24643v1)
- **提交日期**：2026-11-18
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **Comment**：13 pages, 4 figures, 6 tables. Author version of the ACM Multimedia 2026 paper
- **摘要**：Zero-Shot Multimodal Anomaly Detection (ZSMAD) aims to identify both appearance and geometric defects in novel categories without relying on target-specific training data, making it especially valuable in real-world scenarios constrained by data scarcity, privacy concerns, or high annotation costs. While recent approaches successfully adapt vision-language models like CLIP to achieve this open-vocabulary generalization, they still face significant challenges: (i) severe geometric information loss during 3D-to-2D point cloud rendering; (ii) spatially noisy 2D feature maps produced by raw CLIP patch embeddings, compounded by shallow late-stage modality fusion; and (iii) severe cross-modal semantic misalignment due to decoupled processing streams. To address these limitations, we propose AggCLIP, a novel framework that adapts frozen CLIP representations for ZSMAD by explicitly incorporating local geometric structure and visual spatial context. Specifically, to accurately localize fine-grained defects, we design a Geometry-Aware Spatial Aggregation (GSA) module that refines back-projected 3D point features via density-adaptive neighborhood weighting, alongside a Context-Aware Spatial Aggregation (CSA) module that smooths multi-scale 2D patch embeddings using a lightweight trainable adapter. Furthermore, to prevent semantic drift between modalities, we introduce a Dual-Stream Prompt Contrastive (DSPC) loss, which aligns the abstract concepts of normality and abnormality across the point cloud and RGB streams. Extensive experiments demonstrate that the proposed AggCLIP framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on the MVTec 3D-AD and Eyecandies benchmarks under both one-vs-rest and cross-dataset zero-shot settings, notably surpassing the strongest competitive methods by up to 2.9% in pixel-level AUPRO.

## 3. Pathology Randomization for Synthetic Priors and Zero-Shot Detection（0 分）

- **命中**：未命中关键词
- **arXiv**：[2611.21975v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21975v1)
- **提交日期**：2026-11-16
- **分类**：cs.CV, cs.AI, eess.IV
- **Comment**：25 pages, 9 figures, 13 tables (8-page main text with appendices). Code: https://github.com/agr78/Pathology-Randomizer
- **摘要**：Hospital brain imaging archives hold a diverse array of pathology at sample sizes beyond prospective studies, particularly for underrepresented populations and diseases. To unlock uncurated clinical data and scale analysis, methods are needed to detect abnormalities across contrasts and resolutions. Synthetic data has provided robust, contrast-agnostic models for a variety of human neuroimaging tasks in healthy anatomy, but pathology detection and other tasks in clinical settings still require real patient data, contrast-specific models, or disease-specific generators. We propose PathoDraw, a generative model of brain MRI with pathology. A conditional distribution of lesions given the healthy anatomy is drawn into the label map before contrast-agnostic rendering, and a single network estimates healthy anatomy with exact signed intensity deviation of any input scan. The contrast-agnostic 3D UNet learns the healthy labels under the lesion and the signed deviation directly and identifies pathology via robust tissue statistics and learned features, despite no training on real pathology. We show zero-shot detection on public stroke, tumor, and white matter hyperintensity datasets as well as archived clinical data, spanning $T_1$w, FLAIR and $T_2$w at various resolutions under a recent unsupervised anomaly detection benchmark protocol. Our single model exceeds or performs comparably to contrast-specific anomaly detectors trained on real scans and keeps healthy anatomy intact. The same generator trains networks for super-resolution and for registration in the presence of pathology. Our model, robust across acquisition and disease, enables the use of uncurated, archived data for clinical analysis. Our code is available at https://github.com/agr78/Pathology-Randomizer.
